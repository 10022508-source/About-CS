SCREEN TIME

What is the technology behind ScreenTime?

The technology behind screen time would be that whatever device you own has a system or a thing that controls how you interact with your device and shows how much you use your device. Screentime shows how long you have been on an app and tracks when and how long you used the app since you opened it. You can do many things with Screen Time like for example set a time limit on how long you stay on an app.

What is the ethical issue of ScreenTime?

The issue would be if you spend too much time on your devices/screen then it's gonna be bad for your mental health and just you overall. Kids are also doing bad in school because they are on their devices a lot. Some symptoms would be, blurred vision, dry eyes, neck pain, back pain,headache. It wouldn't be a huge issue if people were to give themselves a screen time and wasn't on their devices as much.

Who does this impact more?

This issue impacts a lot of people but this would impact more people who would have hd to use the screen or device 24/7 For example, someone who works somewhere where they use the computer or kids at school who have to use a device for their work all day. Ages 18-39 are the group that impacts more because they feel more of the symptoms.

What are the Trade-offs?

Positives: screen time can bring many positives, some for example would be that it gives good education and can help you learn new things. Another good thing would be how you can play things like video games and other types of apps that can help you grow and apps that can help you in many ways.

Negatives:Although screen time has positive effects it mostly has negative effects. To begin, it can have a huge effect on your mental and physical health if you're on your device too much. It can also affect your sleep schedule and you might not get enough sleep. It could have a huge effect on your eyesight as well.

Recommendations

Some recommendations would be to keep your screen time low and not be on your devices as much, because it's gonna have a bad effect on you. Another recommendation would be to set a screen time so you can keep track of how long you were on your device. Lastly, always remember the bad effects it's gonna have on you if you're on your device a lot.
